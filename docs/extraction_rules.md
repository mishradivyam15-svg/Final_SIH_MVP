# Safety Signal Extraction Rules

Version: 1.0

## Principle

A safety signal is extracted only when the narrative provides
explicit or strongly contextual evidence for that signal.

Unsupported signals remain null.

---

## 1. Hazards

### working_at_height

Strong evidence:
- working on roof
- working on elevated platform
- working on ladder
- working on scaffold
- working on elevated structure
- explicit elevated work location

Do NOT trigger from:
- the word "ladder" alone
- the word "scaffold" alone
- an injury involving a fall without evidence of elevated work

### moving_machinery

Strong evidence:
- rotating machinery
- moving machine
- hand/finger caught in machine
- conveyor/roller/press/lathe movement
- employee interacting with moving machinery

### mobile_equipment

Strong evidence:
- forklift moving/operating
- tractor moving
- golf cart moving
- tug moving
- mobile vehicle/equipment movement

### electrical_energy

Strong evidence:
- energized conductor
- live electrical wire
- electrical shock
- arc flash
- contact with energized electrical component

### line_of_fire

Strong evidence:
- employee in path of moving object
- object shifted/kicked back/rolled and struck employee
- worker exposed to suspended/moving load
- body part caught in movement path

### lifting_operation

Strong evidence:
- lifting load
- rigging
- suspended load
- lifting chain
- crane lifting operation

### excavation

Strong evidence:
- trench
- excavation
- ditch
- excavation collapse
- trench wall collapse

### falling_object

Strong evidence:
- object/rack/material fell and struck employee
- falling equipment/object caused exposure

### slip_trip_fall

Strong evidence:
- employee explicitly slipped
- employee explicitly tripped
- walking surface caused slip/trip/fall

### stored_energy

Strong evidence:
- pressurized energy released
- stored mechanical energy released
- unexpected release of pressure/tension

### chemical_exposure

Strong evidence:
- chemical contacted employee
- chemical splash
- corrosive chemical exposure

## 2. Exposures

### fall_from_height

Strong evidence:
- fell from ladder
- fell from elevated platform
- fell from roof
- fell from second floor
- fell X feet from elevated position

Do NOT trigger from:
- generic "fell" alone

### caught_between

Strong evidence:
- caught between X and Y
- pinched between X and Y
- body part trapped between objects
- pinned between objects

### caught_in

Strong evidence:
- hand/finger/arm caught in machinery
- body part pulled into machinery
- clothing/glove caught in moving equipment

### struck_by

Strong evidence:
- object struck employee
- employee was struck by moving/falling object
- material kicked back and hit employee

### electrical_exposure

Strong evidence:
- electrical shock
- contacted live wire
- contacted energized conductor
- arc flash exposure

### fire_exposure

Strong evidence:
- employee exposed to fire/flames
- vehicle/equipment caught fire and employee was burned

### chemical_exposure

Strong evidence:
- chemical splashed/contacted employee
- employee exposed to corrosive/toxic chemical

### falling_object_exposure

Strong evidence:
- falling object/material struck or pinned employee
